FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

Clear, direct answers about reservations, verified pricing, reviews, and partnerships.

What makes FineTime different from typical booking directories?
+
FineTime is a curated, invitation-first hospitality network. We reject ghost listings and generic scrapers. Each hotel, restaurant, and café on FineTime has an active management relationship, real-time menus with prices in local currency, and verified direct reservation channels.
Can I view full menus and prices before visiting a restaurant?
+
Yes! That is one of FineTime's standout features. By opening any restaurant or café profile on FineTime, you can view the complete digital menu including starters, mains, beverages, dietary labels, and exact prices.
How do table reservations and hotel bookings work?
+
Guests can book tables or reserve room types directly through the FineTime app or web portal. Venues receive instant notifications via their automated FineTime partner systems (including our instant Telegram partner alerts), ensuring rapid confirmation without waiting on unanswered phone calls.
Why does FineTime limit reviews to two per business?
+
To preserve genuine trust. Many platforms suffer from review bombing or fake bot accounts leaving dozens of unverified ratings. FineTime strictly caps accounts at two thoughtful reviews per business, guaranteeing that feedback comes from genuine guests with real experiences.
Is FineTime free for guests and travelers?
+
Yes. Browsing venues, viewing digital menus, checking room rates, reading verified reviews, and making reservations are completely free for all guests on both our website and mobile application.
How can a hotel, restaurant, or lounge become a FineTime partner?
+
Hospitality owners and managers can apply directly by contacting our partner onboarding team at support@finetime.cc or visiting our partner portal. We review each application to ensure ambiance, culinary quality, and service standards meet our guest criteria.
Where can I download the FineTime Android app?
+
You can download the official Android APK directly from our releases page by tapping "Download APK" on this website, or use the high-performance Web App right in any mobile or desktop browser without installation.
